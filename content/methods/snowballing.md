# Snowballing

1. Pick 3 to 5 seed papers you trust.
2. **Backward:** read their reference lists and keep the relevant ones.
3. **Forward:** find papers that cite the seeds.
4. Repeat until a round adds nothing new.

Stop criterion: write it down before you start, so the search ends by rule and not by fatigue.
